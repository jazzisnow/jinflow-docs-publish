What DataDesk gave us

Select a cloud of points in one plot, and they light up everywhere at once. Drag a brush, and every view answers.

This was Tukey's idea made kinetic — Exploratory Data Analysis, 1977: look at the data before you model it. Plots over p-values. Curiosity first. DataDesk let you feel your data move.

No successor

Nothing took its place

Four decades on, no modern tool kept the linked, brushed, EDA-first way of seeing. You've been an expat ever since.

One laptop

It ends with the hardware

Your method, your muscle memory, your way of looking — all trapped on one aging machine. When it goes, they go.

The hope

jinflow is built to be its successor — the same way of seeing, alive beyond one machine.

We kept the soul on purpose. The vocabulary is DataDesk's: the Brush (the gesture), the Selection (the live set), the Selector (the filter you keep).
