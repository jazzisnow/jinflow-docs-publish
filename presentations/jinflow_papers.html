Everything written down, in one place

jinflow is a declarative analytical engine: you declare what should be detected, and it compiles, runs and packages the answer with its provenance attached. These are the documents that explain it — what it is and why, where it is going, and the arguments still being had.

Each entry says how current it is. Anything marked older was written before parts of the system changed and may describe a version that no longer exists — a real risk we would rather flag than have you discover.
