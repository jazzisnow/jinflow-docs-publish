Why this exists, and who it is for

The third of three: one deck says what jinflow is and why it is shaped that way, one says where it is going, and this one says why it should exist at all — who buys it, what they lose without it, and what we are asking for.

It is not an investment pitch, and the last slide is not a raise. What we want is collaboration partners, domain partners, and early adopters with real data and real questions. If money comes up it will be because one of those went well.

01Who it is for

Three motions, and they buy different things

WhoWhat they are actually buying
Public sector
hospital finance · cantonal IT		 Confidence in numbers they already report. The data exists and the questions exist; what is missing is a systematic path from something looks wrong to here is why, and what to do — and a record of how the answer was reached that survives an audit and a staff change
Research
institutes · individual scientists		 Declared analyses instead of a folder of scripts, typed measurements instead of unlabelled numbers, and a single portable store you can freeze, cite and reopen years later and still get the same result
Domain partners
a company that knows a field		 The engine, so their expertise becomes a product rather than a repeated engagement. They know the domain; we carry the machinery; the pack they build is the thing they sell their work around

The first two are end users, mostly public sector. The third is a channel — and it is the one that scales, because a domain partner brings a market we would otherwise have to learn from scratch.

02The alternative

There is no competitor to point at. There is still an alternative.

We are not aware of anyone doing this in the field we operate in. That can change quickly and we would rather say so than build a slide around it — but it does mean the honest comparison is not with a rival product.

The alternative is the status quo, and it is already expensive. It simply never appears on an invoice.

What an organisation that does not do this carries instead: a quality gap they cannot see the size of, and a risk that stays exactly where it was — bad data reaching a decision with nothing in the path designed to catch it. Both are absorbed quietly, which is precisely why they persist. Nobody signs off on them.

That is also the honest limit of the pitch: this is rarely a purchase that replaces a line item. It is a purchase that reduces an exposure, and the buyer has to already believe the exposure is real. In our experience, finance and IT leadership in public institutions usually do — they have met the consequence.

03Why now

A whole domain used to be a project. It is now a few weeks.

Two things changed at once, and both of them are recent enough that the answer would have been different a year ago.

  • Building the framework got radically cheaper. An analytical framework for a new field — the data contract, the extractors, the signals, the expert knowledge — used to be measured in years of specialist work. Ten domain packs now exist, built alongside a single engine, by one person.
  • The same technology helps do the work inside it. Not only writing the code: proposing the model, catching the inconsistency, keeping a vocabulary straight across hundreds of declarations. That is the part that used to need a room of people who all remembered the same things.

What follows from that is a strategy, not just a capability. When trying a market costs weeks instead of years, the sensible move is to try several — and we think there are several, not one niche. Which of them is the market, we do not know yet. Being able to find out cheaply is the whole point, and we would rather say that plainly than draw a hockey stick under a market we have not tested.

04What it is

Three things, and only one of them is “an analytics tool”

The engineDeclared analyses, compiled and run

You declare what should be detected; it compiles to SQL, runs against the data, and produces an answer carrying its own provenance. A new question is a declaration, not a project.

The keeperDomain knowledge, held by the organisation

What the veteran knows — the workaround, the quirk, the event that explains the anomaly — is captured with its author, its scope and its reason. It stops leaving when people do. This is the part no data model can derive and no consultant hands back.

The platformA view that keeps widening

Not a fixed report. New questions, new sources, new breadth on the same foundation — which is why the second year with a tenant looks different from the first without anything being rebuilt.

And one ambition we hold ourselves to: software good enough that people use it because they want to, and the organisational insight arrives as a by-product of them enjoying it. That is why the surface gets as much care as the engine.

05The business

We sell subscriptions. Partners sell the work.

jinflow is sold as a software subscription. We do not sell the analysis, the onboarding or the consulting around it — partners do, and that is deliberate rather than a stage we intend to grow out of.

Which makes one sentence on our public site load-bearing: the pack is a head start; the analysis is the work. Installing a domain pack gives a new tenant a working framework on day one — the contract, the extractors, twenty-odd signals, a catalogue of expert knowledge. Fitting it to this organisation's data is a month of analytical work, and somebody has to do it: their own people, or a partner who does this for a living.

Saying that before a contract rather than after is not modesty. It is the difference between a customer who budgets for the first month and a customer who feels misled in week three — and it is the reason a domain partner has a business at all.

06The askWhat would help

Three kinds of person, and none of them is an investor

CollaborationBuild alongside us

People who will argue with the design and use the thing while it is still moving. The decision documents are published for exactly this reason — the arguments are readable, and they are better with more people in them.

Domain partnerCarry a field

A company that knows an industry and wants their expertise to become a product. They shape the pack, they keep the client work; we carry the engine underneath it. Ten packs exist; not one of them needed us to become domain experts first.

Early adopterA real dataset, a real question

An end user willing to be the first person who is not us — and to say plainly what breaks. The roadmap names this as the one thing on it we cannot do alone.

We are not raising. If capital becomes the right conversation it will be because one of the three above went well and there is something obvious to accelerate — which is a better reason than a deck.
