Capability · see the whole network live

Colour by pollutant. Scrub through time. Read magnitude, not a threshold.

The Selector

Switch the pollutant colouring the map — NO₂, O₃, PM₂.₅, PM₁₀, SO₂, CO — and every station recolours at once.

The Clock

A time slider walks the map through the record: watch winter haze flare and clear, station by station.

The Band Grid

Against the strict guideline nearly every station exceeds. So colour by how many times over — and PM₂.₅ crosses its annual guideline at 255 of 260 stations.

Capability · from overview to evidence live

Outlook · beyond SQL on the roadmap

The Processor — bring your own science into the Study.

So far the engine reasons in SQL. The Processor opens the store to methods SQL can't express — the first being PMF (positive matrix factorization), the workhorse of aerosol source apportionment.

Point it at a speciated matrix, and factor the mixture into its sources — inside the Study, with the same provenance, units, and reproducibility as everything else. Your method becomes a first-class citizen, not an export to a script that leaves no trail.
