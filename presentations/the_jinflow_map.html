Where we actually are

Consolidation is real, and it is measurable

Every sister branch is fully merged into main; main sits one release commit ahead of all four. That is not a coincidence of timing. It is the end of a six-week motion that was almost entirely subtraction.

Read the commit titles from the last three weeks and they say the same thing over and over. The justfile called seven compilers that no longer exist. Forty-one of sixty-four documented recipes were gone. Two hundred forty-eight published commands named a verb the CLI does not have. The Dagster lane had been dead since the pack repos split out in February and was still pulling three packages into every CI run. The Windows installer had been deferred for eight months and was validating manifests it could not build. A demo-video workflow added in March had never once succeeded.

None of that is new capability. All of it is the system learning what it actually is. The two defect shapes this codebase names — green that cannot fail, and two things wearing one word — were the instruments, and they found a great deal.

The engine now describes itself accurately. That is the thing you cannot buy later, and it is what makes the next move cheap.

The shape of the work

Where each lane attaches

The four lanes are not four projects. They attach at four different points on one signal path, which is why they rarely collide and why a decision in one of them rarely unblocks another.

OPS — the Strata substrate done sister-3

BuiltOne SQLite/WAL file per tenant. The engine owns the schema in exactly one place and the app only writes rows. Notes folded in, Selectors and Desks reset, the cloud D1 leg converging on three honest states. jin ops oversized names rows a size-capped cloud will refuse before it refuses them. Residency shipped, and residency.ops was ruled out of existence rather than deferred.

The one thingThe OPS is the one store a rebuild cannot reproduce. That is its design rather than a defect, and its recovery is a restore: an online backup and its deliberate counterpart are both built and documented. What is missing is the routine — nothing invokes them on a schedule, so recovery is only as good as the last time someone remembered. Twelve lived citizens are re-derivable from nowhere, and a saved selection is one of them: it becomes git-backed only when it is promoted, so the first rung of the authoring ladder below stands on this store. Brush all week, promote on Friday, and a Thursday loss takes the week. A scheduled backup is a precondition of the onboarding claim, not hygiene behind it.

Separately, and it is a different kind of question: the overlay citizens — the Bell, bookmarks, what's-new — are still waiting on the publish-seam decision, and the Roster design landed in July with the code still pending. That is a serving-path gap, not a data-loss one, and this paper had them sharing a sentence.

DAW — post-gold processing spine live sister-5, from sister-7 on 4 Nov

BuiltPlan, stem address, render, one Run per stem, and jin topo --dynamic flipping an edge from never_built to fresh through a real OPS round trip. Forty-two tests. The stem content-address is one shared function — the writer imports it, and the reader does not exist yet. The test that will hold the reader to that same function is the one resting skip: a tripwire for the recompute work to arm, not a guard standing watch today. This paper first called it a gate; sister-5 corrected it, and a skipped test cannot go red.

The one thingThe stem body is a no-op. The plumbing is proven with an empty payload, deliberately, because the plugin I/O model is slice-1 work and has not started. This is the lane with the most finished skeleton and the least muscle. It needs one real plugin to stop being an architecture.

EDA — DataDesk reborn has an audience sister-7

BuiltThe Selection is an ambient bus. Brush a map, walk to a dashboard, and the scoping has already travelled. Tiles, charts and tables all sink to one live set of ids, scoped by traversing declared Typology foreign keys rather than hand-written SQL. Cross-entity projection ships. The Inquiry ships, giving the question a home and completing inquiry → thesis → verdict.

The one thingThis is the only lane with a market rather than an architecture. There is an audience of scientists and analysts whose tool was discontinued and who found no successor. They are not waiting for a feature. The open work is the island dissolution — and this paper first called that housekeeping, which sister-7 corrected: the MapCanvas/Lens “island” is the live cross-pack Board/Lens subsystem, shipped and pack-seeded, so retiring it is a migration still needing its spec. Read this lane with the ambition below rather than apart from it: the instruments described here are the authoring surface the whole product reaches through.

Fleet specced, not built sister-9 and sister-1

BuiltThe Fleet Console exists and is gated once for the whole surface. Census, vitals, pack rollups, fleet admins, the shared Codex. The invariant holds: enumerate and classify, never join across tenants.

The one thingThe word does four jobs — authority scope, observability object, credential boundary, and broadcast address — and the code shipped four times against a model that was never settled. The respec's deck landed in four versions, and the rulings landed in a single morning on 2026-09-10. The spec went to its seventh section a month later. What is missing is the register itself — no table, no writer, no register verb — so routing has served static hosts since it shipped. This is the lane where the paper is finished and the code is not.

Video unowned no sister

Where it standsThe March scaffolding was deleted yesterday. It had never run successfully once: it drove scripts that do not exist in this repo, against a config file that has never existed in its history, with a commented-out Postgres service attached to a DuckDB product. It went red six times in eight days and was switched off in the Actions UI, which is why the corpse stayed quiet for eight months.

The one thingThe replacement is already half-built and nobody has noticed. See below.

The thread we left open

A demo video is a Docent tour, recorded

When the scaffolding came out, the note said that a real demo video would be a fresh spec against Playworks, because Playworks actually drives this app. That was right and it was incomplete. The harder half of a demo video is not the recording. It is deciding what to show, in what order, and what to say about each screen. That artifact already exists.

A Docent tour is an ordered list of real JinDesk routes, each carrying a title and a why in three locales, each able to arrive with a Selection already lit, and each making a machine-checkable claim about the data through the Warrant. Thirteen tour files are declared on disk — eleven distinct tours across ten of the thirteen tenants. Three of those tenants share one pack starter, which is why the unit of recording is a tour and a tenant, not a tour.

So the demo video is not a new artifact. It is a renderer over an existing one:

The same recorder serves three audiences at once. Sales gets a current product video per pack. A new user gets a narrated first visit. The canary gets another surface it is already most of the way to covering.

Look at which names sit in which band. The Senses in bloom are the craft ones: how a page composes, how a column declares its type, how config cascades, how a tour narrates, how the three stores divide. Those are answered and alive.

The Senses still forming are, almost without exception, the commercial ones. The Fleet is how you operate many customers. The Broker is how you license and authenticate one who never connects. The Partner is how somebody else's framework runs inside your engine. The Ground is where it all sits.

Jinflow knows how to do the work. It has not yet decided how a stranger buys it, installs it, or authors in it.

That is not a criticism of the sequencing. Craft first was right, and the consolidation proved the craft is sound. It is a statement about where the next marginal hour buys the most.

The ambition

Up and running, without a Claude account

You set this as the extra mile. It is the right target, and it is closer than it looks, because the hardest part is already shipped and is being counted in a different column.

The honest statement of the gap: jinflow can already be run without Claude. It cannot yet be authored without Claude. And authoring is what matters, because the pack is the product. Every pack that exists was authored by you with a sister. No outsider has ever written one.

RUNG 1

Run it without Claude

prove it

Install, jin init, drop CSVs, jin make, jin desk. That path does not touch an AI at any point and has not for a long time. What is missing is evidence. Nobody has ever done a cold start on a clean machine following only the published documentation, and the Front Door audit tells you what would have happened if they had: a reader who types a command that does not exist stops there. Sixty-nine such commands are still live.

The deliverable is a test, not a document. A cold-start gate in CI: fresh container, install from the published instructions, scaffold a tenant, build it, serve it, run Playworks green. It is exactly the kind of guard this codebase has learned to build the right way round, and it turns the onboarding claim into something that can go red.

RUNG 2

Author without Claude

half shipped

This is the one that looked impossible and is not. The First Citizen shipped in August and went green end to end against a real tenant with a 125-finding run. Brush a cohort in the EDA instruments. Save it as a predicate-backed Selector. Press promote. The predicate is translated into a signal population, the Signal Builder is seeded, and what comes out the other side is an ordinary compiled signal.

A domain expert who has never seen YAML and does not know what a thesis is can produce a working analytical artifact by gesture. That is the authoring path that does not need an AI, and it was built inside the EDA lane where it has been filed under exploration rather than under onboarding.

You do not write a signal. You brush one.

What is left is the rest of the diamond. A signal can be gestured; a thesis, a verdict and Subject Matter still want prose. The Atelier is the home for those and it is still forming — a tabbed editor over artifact types, specced in May, with the Inquiry having independently landed its own first-class surface and proved the shape was right. Finishing the Atelier is the second half of this rung.

And the claim above overreaches by one word, which the sister who built the First Citizen says plainly: it is the answer to gesturing a signal without Claude, not yet to authoring without Claude. What breaks first under a stranger is not in her lane but in this one — and the sister who owns that store has narrowed it again: the membrane is built, a per-operator draft buffer that waits for a deliberate publish, and it is the First Citizen's save that routes around it rather than through it. A mis-brushed cohort becomes a standing diagnostic with nothing in between, but the fix is a routing change, not a gate built from nothing. Then: only a predicate-backed selection may promote, so the most natural first gesture meets a refusal where the interface should teach. The honest claim is smaller and still carries the rung — authoring by gesture is proven possible, and what remains is precisely the stranger-facing part.

RUNG 3

Claude as an upgrade, not a dependency

join two specs

When AI is in the loop it should be brokered, never bring-your-own-key. Asking a hospital to open an Anthropic account and paste a key into your product is a sale you will lose on the procurement call alone.

Both halves are already written. The Broker says jinflow.io answers two questions on one handshake — who is this, and are they entitled to run — and returns a signed pass the local install verifies offline. The Agent Profile says the model and the key resolve through a three-scope cascade, personal over tenant over deployment, with Vera asking once when two exist.

Put them together and inference entitlement is one more field in a pass the customer already needs for their licence. The key lives at jazzisnow, metered and revocable. The customer never holds one, never creates an account, and Vera works on first launch. That is the extra mile, and it is a joining exercise rather than an invention.

If it were mine to sequence

What I would do next

  1. The cold-start gate

    Cheapest item here and it protects the largest claim. Converts "a stranger can install this" from prose into CI. Do it first because everything below is easier to judge once it exists.

  2. The Docent recorder

    Eleven tours declared across thirteen tenant pairings, no renderer. One recorder serves sales, onboarding and the canary, reuses two shipped systems, and is the only item on this list that produces something you can show someone outside the building this month.

  3. Build the Fleet store — a sister's, not yours

    The deck exists in four versions and the rulings landed on 2026-09-10. What is missing is the register they ruled on: no table, no writer, no register verb, so routing has served static hosts since it shipped. Two narrow confirmations sit with you — whether a Site holds presences, and whether the aspect must be spoken at a broadcast call site — and neither blocks the build.

  4. Give the DAW a payload

    One real plugin through the proven spine. Until a stem contains something, the lane is forty-two tests of excellent plumbing with nothing flowing through it, and there is no way to tell whether the model is right.

  5. Spec the Broker × Agent Profile join

    Doc before code, as the house rule says, because this one is a commercial decision wearing a technical shape. It is the extra mile, and it is also the thing that decides what jinflow costs to run per customer.

One item sits outside that order because it is a prerequisite rather than a move. Everything else on this page can be recovered from git or rebuilt from the AFS; the OPS is the one store that cannot, which is why its recovery is a restore. That restore is built. Putting it on a schedule is not, and that is the item — small, and ahead of anything that puts more weight on the store.
