A typed vocabulary

Building blocks with precise definitions.

Signal
A typed observation — polarity, score, direction, impact.
Thesis
A business question answered by signals and perspectives.
Verdict
A root-cause judgment with confidence and recommendation.
Observation
A signed strategic finding at the top of the taxonomy.
Subject Matter
Atomic expert knowledge — statements and executable checks.
Dossier
Curated narratives that group Subject Matter into stories.

Each term has one meaning. Each one composes. The vocabulary travels across every domain pack unchanged.

The shape

Workspace
Live in-progress capture — notebook cells, comments, work-in-flight before it earns its place in the durable record.

The materialised analytical output — queryable, baked, auditable, ready for the lens that reads it.

Framework drives the build. Workspace feeds it. Knowledge is what readers query.

Degrees of freedom

If you wish so.

  • Cloud-free, if you wish. Run completely on your laptop or your own hardware. Offline-capable — just refresh your login every 30 days.
  • AI-free, if you wish. The framework works without an LLM ever entering the room. Use AI when it helps; you never depend on it.
  • Deterministic, even when you do use AI. evolve helps you author the framework. The engine executes the framework. The data you see is the result of pure, reproducible processing — with or without AI in the loop.
