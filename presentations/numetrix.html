What hospitals can't see

Hospital billing leakage is real. Most of it is invisible to the team that produces the bills.

Materials used, not billed

A surgeon scans an implant. It never lands on the invoice. Departments lose CHFs every day with no audit trail.

Duplicate billing, opposite signs

The same line appears in usage and again as an inventory adjustment. Net zero on the books — real money in flight.

Catalogues age, prices drift

A material's price was updated in SAP but not in OPALE. The wrong number rides every transaction for months.

How nuMetrix works

Diagnostic signals running on your operational data — every night, with provenance.

Source-system agnostic

OPALE, SAP MM, SAP IS-H, Navision. We map their columns to one canonical model. Your data stays where it is.

Signals as evidence

Each signal is a YAML question: "What's the imbalance between usage and billing for material X this month?" Compiled to SQL, validated against the contract.

Verdicts close the loop

When a thesis confirms — "we are losing money on unbilled implants" — the system explains why and what to do.
