How it works

AFS in. KLS out. One make.

AFS — Artifact Store

Git-versioned YAML. Signals detect anomalies, theses ask business questions, verdicts explain why. Validated end-to-end before any SQL runs.

KLS — Knowledge Store

One DuckDB file. Signed snapshots, self-describing, immutable. The build product travels with its source.

Five operating modes

Local, peer-to-peer-to-people, cloud, semi-cloud, make-as-a-service. Same code converges. Sovereignty by construction.
